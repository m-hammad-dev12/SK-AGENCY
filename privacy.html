Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website or contact us via our enquiry forms, WhatsApp, or telephone, we collect information you voluntarily provide. This includes your name, phone number, email address, selected service or product category, and details included in your enquiry message.

2. How We Use Your Information

SK AGENCY uses the information collected strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • To review and respond to your service requests and product enquiries.
  • To facilitate communication regarding car key assistance, appliance repair coordination, or product sourcing.
  • To connect you with qualified service professionals or providers appropriate to your request.
  • To provide direct customer support via phone, email, or WhatsApp.

3. Data Protection & Confidentiality

We take appropriate administrative and technical measures to protect your contact information. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies. Contact details are shared solely to the extent necessary to coordinate and fulfill your requested service.

4. Third-Party Links & WhatsApp Communication

Our website includes direct links to initiate conversations on WhatsApp. Any interactions conducted via WhatsApp are governed by WhatsApp's terms of service and privacy policy. We encourage you to review their terms when using the messaging application.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have questions about our privacy practices, wish to update your information, or would like to withdraw an enquiry, please contact us directly at info@skagency.pk or call +92 370 1604764.
